Real-world side quests

Your city, as someone else.

A SideQuest is a short real-world route told as chapters, lived as a persona: a 1950s novelist, an urban detective, a waterfront poet. This agent keeps it alive while you're out.

  1. Get a SideQuest. Load the demo on the right (a struggling novelist in Greenwich Village).
  2. Tell it what changed. "The gallery is closed and it's raining", "my budget is now $10", "I don't want to walk that far".
  3. Only the affected chapters change. Same persona, same story. Every tool call shows up as a ticket here, and the route on the right redraws.
